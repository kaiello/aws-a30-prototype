# Table D1: New Terms and Definitions Added in AWS A3.0M/A3.0:2020

*Location: AWS A3.0M/A3.0:2020 Annex D, pp. 165–166 (PDF pp. 177–178)*

Over 120 new technical terms and definitions were introduced in the 2020 edition:

### Waveform & Electrical Terminology
* **average instantaneous power (AIP)**: The average value of the instantaneous product of welding current and arc voltage calculated over an integer number of intervals.
* **total instantaneous energy (TIE)**: The integral of instantaneous power over a defined interval.
* **waveform-controlled welding, arc welding**: Arc welding with equipment that dynamically modifies the current and voltage output waveform during each cycle to control droplet transfer, heat input, or bead geometry.
* **arc energy (E)**: Energy supplied by the welding arc, often calculated from instantaneous power measurements.
* **welding waveform**: The graphical representation of electrical parameters (voltage, current) variation over time during welding.

### Advanced Manufacturing & Processes
* **additive manufacturing**: The process of joining materials to make parts from 3D model data, usually layer upon layer.
* **advancing side, friction stir welding**: The side of a friction stir weld where the tool rotation vector has the same direction as the tool travel vector.
* **retreating side, friction stir welding**: The side of a friction stir weld where the tool rotation vector is in the opposite direction to the tool travel vector.
* **advancing side, high energy beam welding**
* **retreating side, high energy beam welding**

### Welding Positions & Couplings
* **2F, pipe**: Horizontal fillet welding position for pipe where axis is vertical.
* **3F, pipe**: Vertical fillet welding position for pipe.
* **3G, pipe**: Vertical groove welding position for pipe with vertical axis.
* **4G, pipe**: Overhead groove welding position for pipe.
* **orbital welding**: Automatic or machine welding of tubes or pipes in which the electrode rotates around the workpiece.
* **out-of-position welding**: Welding performed in positions other than the flat position.

### Joint Geometry & Boundaries
* **parallel joint**: A joint between two or more members where the members are parallel and have planar or cylindrical interfaces. (Replaced nonstandard 'edge joint').
* **butting workpiece**: A workpiece that abuts another member forming a joint interface.
* **nonbutting workpiece**: A workpiece in a joint that does not form an abutting interface.
* **reentrant angle**: An angle formed between the weld face and the base metal surface.
* **toe reentrant angle**: Reentrant angle specifically measured at the weld toe.
* **bead reentrant angle**: Reentrant angle between adjacent weld beads in a multipass weld.
* **end dam**: A device placed at the end of a joint to contain the weld pool (ESW/EGW).
* **shelf bar**: A backing member used to support weld metal.
* **sump, ESW and EGW**: A depression or runoff area at the top of an electroslag or electrogas weld.

### Temper Bead Terminology
* **temper bead**: A weld bead placed at a specific location to thermally temper previously deposited weld metal or the heat-affected zone.
* **temper bead sequence**: A specific programmed sequence of depositing temper beads.
* **temper pass**: A pass consisting of temper beads.
* **toe temper bead**: A temper bead placed along the weld toe to temper the base metal HAZ.
* **toe temper pass**: A pass of toe temper beads.

### Qualification & Governance
* **performance qualification**: Verification that a welder, welding operator, brazer, or solderer can produce sound joints.
* **performance qualification test record (PQTR)**: The documented record of a qualification test.
* **performance qualification variable**: A variable whose change requires requalification.
* **welding procedure qualification variable**: An essential or nonessential variable documented in a PQR.
