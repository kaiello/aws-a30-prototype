# Table D6: Deleted Terms in AWS A3.0M/A3.0:2020

*Location: AWS A3.0M/A3.0:2020 Annex D, p. 170 (PDF p. 182)*

The 2020 edition deleted 29 obsolete, redundant, or chemistry terms from the standard:

### Deleted Chemistry and Physical Science Terms
*(Removed because general physics and chemistry definitions are governed by standard scientific dictionaries rather than welding standard definitions)*
* **covalent bond** (Deleted from standard)
* **ionic bond** (Deleted from standard)
* **metal** (Deleted from standard)
* **differential thermal expansion** (Deleted from standard)

### Deleted Redundant Process & Electrical Terms
* **automatic arc welding current**
* **automatic arc welding downslope time**
* **automatic arc welding upslope time**
* **brazed joint** (Redundant with joint definition)
* **brittle nugget**
* **carbon arc brazing**
* **direct welding, resistance welding**
* **electroslag welding electrode**
* **fill weld**
* **forge-delay time, resistance welding**
* **full fillet weld**
* **incomplete bond**
* **indirect welding, resistance welding**
* **parallel welding**
* **pulse, resistance welding**
* **random intermittent welds**
* **reaction stress**
* **resistance welding current**
* **resistance welding downslope time**
* **resistance welding time**
* **resistance welding upslope time**
* **selective block sequence**
* **series welding**
* **weld time**
* **welder registration**
