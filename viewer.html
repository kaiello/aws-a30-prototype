### 1F, Pipe Welding Test Position

The **1F, Pipe** designation is a welding test position for a **circumferential fillet weld** applied to a joint in pipe. In this position, the pipe's axis is approximately 45° from horizontal, and the weld is made in the **flat welding position** by rotating the pipe about its axis.
